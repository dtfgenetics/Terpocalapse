# Level 01 — The Veg Lab

## Purpose

The Veg Lab is the first playable teaching level for Terpocalypse. It introduces movement, shooting, pickups, keycards, doors, enemies, secrets, and the level exit.

## Player Experience Goals

- Player learns movement in a safe starting room.
- Player finds the pH Blaster quickly.
- Player fights the first Spider Mite Swarm.
- Player collects health/ammo.
- Player sees a locked green door before finding the green keycard.
- Player finds a simple secret stash room.
- Player exits after surviving a small enemy encounter.

## Room Flow

1. Start Room
2. Grow-Light Corridor
3. First Pest Room
4. Nutrient Storage
5. Green Keycard Room
6. Locked Green Door
7. Secret Stash Room
8. Exit Chamber

## Enemy Placement

- Start Room: none
- Grow-Light Corridor: one Spider Mite Swarm
- First Pest Room: two Spider Mite Swarms
- Nutrient Storage: one Powdery Mildew Ghoul
- Exit Chamber: one Nute Burn Imp

## Pickup Placement

- pH Blaster near start
- Cure Jar Health after first enemy room
- Light Ammo Box in Nutrient Storage
- Green Keycard in Green Keycard Room
- Kief Armor in Secret Stash Room

## Visual Theme

- green grow light glow
- concrete floor
- reflective mylar wall sections
- wet floor patches
- warning decals
- nutrient tanks
- clone trays
- fans
- pipes

## Completion Condition

Player reaches the exit switch/door after opening the green keycard door and defeating or avoiding the final room enemy.
