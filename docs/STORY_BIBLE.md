# Terpocalypse Story Bible

## Core Premise

A hidden cultivation research facility has gone wrong. The grow rooms, clone labs, cure vaults, nutrient systems, and seed storage wings are contaminated by runaway pests, mold blooms, unstable nutrients, and malfunctioning security systems.

The player is the last grower still inside the facility. Their mission is to secure the seed vault, stop the contamination from spreading, and recover the genetics before the entire grow room goes under.

## Player Role

The player is known as the Seed Runner.

The Seed Runner is not a soldier. They are a grower, fixer, and survivor who knows the facility layout, understands the cultivation systems, and has to improvise with grow-room tools to make it out alive.

## Main Objective

Reach the Seed Vault and secure the genetics core.

## Story Tone

- retro action horror
- cannabis cultivation parody
- grow-room disaster comedy
- tense but playful
- original THC/DTF game identity

## Story Rules

- Keep the story cannabis cultivation themed.
- Do not make the game about consuming cannabis.
- Do not make it a sales tool.
- Focus on grow-room chaos, pests, mold, nutrient failure, genetics, seed vaults, and survival.
- Keep humor in logs, signs, and item names.
- Keep the main mission clear and playable.

## Opening Setup

The facility alarm goes off during a late-night inspection. The clone racks are misting too hard, the nutrient system is overheating, the cure vault is sealed, and the seed bank is locked behind emergency security.

The Seed Runner wakes up near the Veg Lab with only basic tools and a bad feeling. The facility AI keeps repeating one message:

Secure the Seed Vault.

## Main Mystery

The player slowly learns that the contamination is not one problem. It is a chain reaction:

- pests broke containment
- mold spread through ventilation
- nutrient tanks overheated
- security drones misread growers as contamination
- the genetics vault sealed itself
- the Mold Mother formed near the seed vault core

## Ending Goal

At the end of Episode One, the player reaches the Seed Vault, clears the contamination source, and secures the genetics core.

The ending should leave room for future episodes:

- outdoor grow zone
- dispensary district
- underground breeder bunker
- corporate lab takeover
- seed vault expansion
